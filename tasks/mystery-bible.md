# Case Bible — "LAST ONE OUT" (neo-noir office murder)

The office is only the setting. The story is a murder — nothing to do with the firm's business.

## Spine
- **Victim:** SAM (quant). Found dead on the coworking/bullpen floor. Staged to read as a collapse/fall.
- **Killer (truth):** DAVID (founder) — the man who pulls the player in and wants it ruled an accident before first shift (06:00).
- **Motive (personal, NOT corporate):** Years ago David killed a man in a hit-and-run and bought silence. Sam found the proof and had been quietly blackmailing him. Sam pushed once too hard tonight; David killed them, took back the proof, scrubbed his badge entry, staged the scene, and is steering the intern toward a patsy.
- **The patsy:** PONCHO (owed Sam money; twitchy; his jacket/IOU near the scene). David + Dena point you at him. Innocent.
- **Player:** the night-desk intern who found the body. Deadline: 06:00 first shift (HUD "SUNRISE 06:00").

## Cast (6 living suspects + victim; Jamie removed)
- **David** — founder, the killer. Calm, paternal, lethal. In his office. Steers you to Poncho; lies he left at midnight; turns cold if pressed.
- **Sam** — victim. Voice lives only in the evidence.
- **Jay** — engineer, the conscience/ally. Saw David & Sam argue in the office ~midnight ("years ago"). In coworking.
- **Peem** — designer, earwitness. Heard the thud ~02:00 and saw a calm figure walk to the elevator. At the printer.
- **Dena** — office ops, loyal/unwitting accomplice. Tidied the scene on David's word; misdirects to Poncho. In pantry.
- **Poncho** — analyst, the patsy. Owed Sam (debt forgiven); was stuck in the dead elevator. In elevator.
- **Ching** — producer, red herring. Sam blackmailed Ching too (personal); but Ching was fleeing town, not killing. On the sofa.

## Clue web (TRUE_CULPRIT = david). 9 clues; accusation picks 3; Ending A needs >=2 CORE/HEAVY vs David.
Examine (one per room):
- coworking `the-body` CORE [david] — staged scene; Sam's phone (the leverage) is gone → premeditated homicide.
- office `retrieved-proof` HEAVY [david] — the blackmail item back in David's drawer.
- pantry `two-glasses` CORE [david] — two drinks at 2am, one wiped of prints.
- printer `shredded-letter` HEAVY [david] — the proof Sam printed to confront him, shredded.
- elevator `scrubbed-badge` HEAVY [david] — log shows Sam #D2 in; David's #D1 entry wiped.
- sofa `owed-note` WEAK [poncho] — Poncho's IOU. RED HERRING.
Dialogue-granted:
- jay → `witness-argument` CORE [david]; peem → `earwitness-thud` CORE [david]; ching → `ching-motive` WEAK [ching] (herring).

David dominates the suspicion meter once examined (it's a truth-tracker). The misdirection is narrative: the authority figures (David, Dena) point at Poncho. The deduction = trust the evidence over the man who hired you.

## Endings (neo-noir, bitter)
- A — name David with hard proof: you nail it, but he'll lawyer out by noon and you're marked. Truth wins, ugly.
- B — David, thin proof: right and unprovable; you're walked out before sunrise.
- C — accuse anyone else (esp. Poncho): you were steered; the real killer walks; the phone goes in the river.
- D — timeout at 06:00: ruled an accident; the body becomes a sad story; you never said the word.
